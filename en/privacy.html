Privacy Policy

Effective: October 4, 2026 · Last updated: November 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the developer of Since ("we," "us") handles information in the iOS app Since (the "App").

Summary
  • Your item names, records, and settings are stored only on your iPhone. They are never sent to our servers (we don't have any servers).
  • There is no account sign-up or sign-in.
  • The App communicates with outside services only to purchase, restore, and verify in-app purchases (Lifetime and subscriptions). Nothing you enter or record is ever sent.
  • We don't use ads, usage analytics, crash reporting, or cross-app tracking.

1. Information kept on your device

The following information that you enter or record in the App is stored and processed only on your device. It is never sent to us or any third party, and we cannot see it.

  • Item names, colors, icons, target intervals, and whether reminders are on
  • Records (the days you did something) and their history
  • Settings such as the reminder time, and whether the App has shown its how-to tips

Days since, the calendar, and Insights are all calculated on your device.

Saved data is stored in a format that can't be read while your device is locked (iOS Data Protection).

2. Information sent to process purchases

To purchase, restore, and verify in-app purchases (Lifetime, Yearly, and Monthly), the App uses RevenueCat, a service provided by RevenueCat, Inc., and Apple's App Store. When it does, the following information is sent to RevenueCat's servers.

Information sent
  • Purchase information (the product purchased, purchase date and time, expiration date, transaction details, and the store's country or region)
  • An anonymous user ID created automatically by RevenueCat (not linked to your name, email address, or similar)
  • The identifier for vendor (IDFV: a device identifier shared only among apps from the same developer)
  • Technical information that comes with the connection (app and OS version, device model, language and region, IP address, SDK version, and similar)
  • The App Tracking Transparency permission status (a value such as "not determined," because the App never asks for permission)
Information not sentAnything you enter or record, including item names, records, settings, and the number of items. Information that identifies you, such as your name or email address.
PurposeTo verify purchases, prevent duplicate purchases, restore purchases, provide paid features, prevent fraud, and compile purchase statistics

When you tap a purchase button on the free plan, the App first sends the purchase information on your device to RevenueCat to check whether you've already purchased with the same Apple Account, so that you aren't charged twice. Only the purchase information listed above is sent, and you are never asked to sign in.

This information is never used for advertising or tracking (following you across other companies' apps or websites). The App doesn't use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and never asks for tracking permission. RevenueCat's diagnostics reporting is turned off.

Payment itself is handled by Apple (the App Store). We never receive payment information such as credit card details.

For how each company handles information, see their privacy policies.

  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Apple Privacy Policy

3. Ads and analytics

The App doesn't use ads, usage analytics, or crash reporting.

4. Notifications

To remind you on the due date of items with a target, the App uses notifications scheduled on your device (local notifications). No outside server is used for notifications. You can change notification permission at any time in the iPhone Settings app.

5. Device permissions

The only device permission the App uses is Notifications. It doesn't access Photos, Location, Contacts, the microphone, the camera, Health, or similar.

6. Sharing with third parties

We don't share your information with third parties except as described in "2. Information sent to process purchases." Because we don't hold anything you enter or record, we can't share it with anyone.

7. Backups

The App's saved data may be included in device backups made by iOS (iCloud Backup or backups to a computer). Backups are managed by Apple and by you, and we can't access their contents.

8. Deleting your information

  • To delete an item, swipe left on its row in the list, or tap "Delete This Item" on the item's edit screen. Deleting an item also deletes all of its records and reminders (you can bring it back with "Undo," shown right after you delete it).
  • To delete a single record, use the list of records on the item's detail screen.
  • Deleting the App from your device deletes all data saved on the device. Deleted data can't be recovered, except by restoring from a device backup.
  • To have purchase information sent to RevenueCat deleted, contact us at the address below.

9. Children

If you are a minor, please use the App, and especially make in-app purchases, with the consent of a parent or guardian.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as needed. If we make important changes, we'll let you know on this page or in the App. The updated policy takes effect when it's posted on this page.
