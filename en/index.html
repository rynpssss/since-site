Since
Know when you last did it

Since is an iPhone app that shows, at a glance, how many days it's been since you last did everyday things like replacing a toothbrush or washing the sheets, and whether it's time to do them again.

When you do something, one tap marks it "Done today." For items with a target, Since reminds you on the due date. The calendar and Insights let you look back at your records.

Your records are stored only on your iPhone and are never sent off the device. No account needed.
